> **How to use this file.** For each new release, copy the most recent
> `## [<version>] - <date>` section from `CHANGELOG.md` below as a starting

> release's entries into it. Keep the groups below; add groups only if no
> existing one fits. Each entry is one line, starts with a capital letter,
> and ends with a period. Do not bump the version yourself — it comes from
> `app/__version__.py` (see `RELEASE.md`).
